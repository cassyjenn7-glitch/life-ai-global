Our Commitments

Trust, Safety and Responsibility

Healthcare technology must put people first. Safety and accountability are central to our development.

🔒 Privacy First

We aim to build with strong privacy protections, appropriate consent and secure handling of personal information.

👩‍⚕️ Human Oversight

AI is intended to support, not replace, qualified healthcare professionals and clinical judgment.

📚 Evidence Matters

Health information should be carefully reviewed, responsibly presented and updated as evidence evolves.

Medical Emergency Notice

LIFE AI Global is not an emergency dispatch service. If you believe someone is experiencing a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department immediately. Do not delay urgent care while using this website.
